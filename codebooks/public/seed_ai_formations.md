# AI26 public codebook — ideological contestation over AI

This is the publication-safe reference codebook for the AI26 study described in
`TomiToivio/LaclauGPT/paper/PAPER.md`. It is public methodology, not a dump of
private corpus-derived labels or researcher annotations.

The codebook is intentionally **non-adjudicative**. It supplies candidate
vocabulary and conceptual anchors to context memory and prompts. Codebook terms
are never source evidence, and matching a seed does not establish a theoretical
role or ideological formation.

## Canonical formations (kind=formation)

The current computational vocabulary is deliberately small so aggregation and
visualization remain stable:

- `accelerationism`
- `doomerism`
- `left-wing accelerationism`
- `ai safety`
- `ai critical`
- `anti-ai`

Definition attached to each seed:

> Canonical AI26 computational formation label used as a PROVISIONAL sensitising
> category. Membership must be supported by source evidence; multi-label overlap,
> uncertainty and abstention are valid. The six labels are reproducibility anchors,
> not a closed ontology and not permanent properties of actors.

The paper's research vocabulary is richer than these six aggregation labels.
Techno-optimism, e/acc, singularitarianism, transhumanism, existential-risk
reasoning, alignment, Critical AI Studies, labour resistance, artist/creator
opposition, environmental resistance and opposition to particular deployments
must not be collapsed automatically into one formation without evidence.

## Arenas

AI26 compares three public research arenas:

1. `elites` — AI labs, entrepreneurs, researchers, intellectuals and adjacent movements;
2. `grassroots` — mobilisation around employment, culture, surveillance, environment,
   data centres, AI safety and opposition/support;
3. `parliamentary` — parliamentary, electoral, party, government and policy discourse.

Arena membership is sampling provenance, not evidence of ideology.

## Seeding and update rules

Seeds enter memory as PROVISIONAL. Promotion or normalization requires the
resolution loop and human validation. New current-event vocabulary should
normally enter as signifiers/tags/motifs rather than expanding the six top-level
formation labels.

Public codebooks may be updated from the public paper, public situation reports
and documented public discourse. Never seed private corpus-derived surface forms,
private source lists, unpublished annotations, credentials or operational
infrastructure into this file.
