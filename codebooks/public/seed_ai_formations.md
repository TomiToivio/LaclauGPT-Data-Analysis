# Seed codebook — AI discourse formations (from the paper)

Seed definitions are deliberately non-adjudicative: research vocabulary may be
richer than the small AI26 computational formation vocabulary. These seeds are
public methodology derived from the paper "Ideological contestation over AI".
Synthetic seed vocabulary only — no corpus-derived content.

## Canonical formations (kind=formation, PROVISIONAL sensitising categories)

`accelerationism`, `doomerism`, `left-wing accelerationism`, `ai safety`,
`ai critical`, `anti-ai`

Definition attached to each seed:

> Canonical AI26 computational formation label used as a provisional
> sensitising category; membership must be evidenced, multi-label overlap is
> allowed, and richer ideological vocabulary belongs in evidence/tags.

## Affect seeds (kind=target, PROVISIONAL)

Each seed carries:

> Candidate affect term; its target, political side, contextual valence,
> and function must be demonstrated from source evidence.

hope · pride · ambition · confidence (Us-side example vocabulary);
resentment · anger · fear · contempt (Frontier-side example vocabulary).
The Us/Frontier listing is illustrative seeding only — affect polarity is
never inferred from side membership (THEORY.md INV_AFFECT).
