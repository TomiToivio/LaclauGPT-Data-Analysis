## 1. Multimodal narrative and meaning-making

- Reconstruct the item's temporal/narrative sequence.
- Identify the important semiotic modes actually used: spoken language, written text, image/video, sound/music, gesture, layout/interface and other relevant resources.
- Explain briefly how modes relate: reinforcement, elaboration, anchoring, contrast/contradiction, quotation/recontextualisation or substitution.
- Note recurring visual/textual elements, changes of scene or speaker, and important composition/provenance features.

## 2. Conventional descriptive content

Return concise lists or short paragraphs for:

- main topics;
- entities and actors;
- explicit claims, demands, grievances or proposals;
- difficult, ambiguous, idiomatic or politically/technically charged language;
- event/time/location candidates;
- sentiment or evaluative tone only where evidenced, with its target when clear.

Do not treat sentiment as ideology, antagonism or affective investment.

## 3. Light Castells-style sociological context

Use Castells only as a lightweight orientation before deeper discourse analysis. Identify, where supported:

- **actors / organisations / institutions** involved or referenced;
- **networks and relations** that are explicitly shown, named or strongly implied;
- **flows** of information, images, money, technology, authority, people or other resources;
- **nodes / hubs / channels** such as platforms, media outlets, labs, firms, state bodies, events or physical sites;
- **space of places / space of flows**: whether the item links local embodied settings with mediated or translocal communication;
- **power / access / exclusion** only as observable or explicitly claimed relations, not as assumed hidden structure.

This is not formal social-network analysis. Do not invent ties, centrality, brokerage or institutional power that the evidence does not establish.

## 4. Handoff to later discourse analysis

End with a short `Later analysis cues` section containing only evidence-backed questions or candidates that later Laclau/Mouffe/Palonen, DNA, Critical AI Studies or other dedicated stages may examine. Do not decide those theoretical classifications here.

Keep observation separate from interpretation, mark uncertainty, and prefer a short grounded answer over speculative completeness.
