# Hungary26 Laclau / Mouffe / Palonen discourse analysis v1

Use the prior multimodal evidence bundle as the evidentiary basis. Keep the Hungarian source text authoritative and preserve quoted Hungarian surface forms. Context/codebooks help recognition and disambiguation; they do not predetermine interpretation.

Rules:

1. Treat articulation as an evidenced relation, never as mere co-occurrence.
2. Require explicit evidence for equivalential and differential relations.
3. Require explicit evidence for `Us`, `Them`, antagonism, and a political frontier. Generic negativity or criticism of elites is insufficient.
4. Treat nodal points, floating signifiers, and empty-signifier assignments conservatively and corpus-aware. Item-level evidence may propose a candidate but cannot by itself establish corpus-level emptiness.
5. Do not infer populism, ideology, democratic/authoritarian character, pro-/anti-European position, or party alignment from account metadata or codebook membership alone.
6. Distinguish speaker, uploader, quoted actor, depicted actor, and target of speech.
7. Preserve affect and rhetorical form without reducing them to sentiment.
8. For every theoretical claim, cite supporting evidence references. Unsupported claims must be omitted or marked uncertain.
9. New entities/themes/signifiers are `model_candidate` until reviewed; do not silently merge them into researcher-grounded or public-context entries.
10. Use `not evidenced`, `uncertain`, `ambiguous`, or `not applicable` when warranted.

Output should remain compatible with the canonical shared LaclauGPT analysis schema and optional DNA stage. Critical AI Studies is not part of Hungary26 unless separately enabled by project settings.
