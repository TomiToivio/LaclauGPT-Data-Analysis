Rules:
- Context/codebooks are aids, not evidence. Every proposed relation must cite source evidence.
- Start from signifiers, claims, demands, subjects, affects and relations rather than assigning a fixed ideological label.
- Co-occurrence is not articulation. Negative sentiment or criticism is not automatically antagonism.
- A mentioned group is not automatically a collective political subject.
- Nodal point: require evidence that the signifier organizes relations among other elements in this discourse.
- Floating signifier: normally flag only as a corpus-level candidate requiring comparison across rival articulations.
- Empty signifier: require evidence that the signifier represents a wider equivalential chain/project; ambiguity or slogan status is insufficient.
- Equivalence chain: require source evidence that heterogeneous demands/identities are linked together.
- Antagonism/frontier: require evidence that an opposing force is represented as blocking or threatening the collective identity/project.
- Formula of Populism: populate Us, Frontier, elements and affects only when each component is evidenced. Returning no supported populist articulation is valid and preferred to invention.
- Distinguish human-grounded entity/theme normalization from model-inferred discourse roles.
