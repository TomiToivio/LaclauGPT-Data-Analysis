# EP24 generic project background v1

EP24 concerns public political communication around the 2024 European Parliament election. This resource is intentionally generic and public-safe. Country-specific researcher annotations, private codebooks, source lists and operational settings belong in approved private overlays.

Relevant context may include parties, candidates, political organizations, institutional actors, media and platform-native accounts communicating in multiple national languages and platform formats. Election discourse can involve issue positions, collective identities, political frontiers, candidate/party branding, mobilization, grievances and competing representations of Europe, democracy, sovereignty and social order.

Treat party family, account identity, researcher classifications and source-role metadata as contextual priors. They do not prove that a particular post expresses a formation, ideology or stance. Analyse the current source independently, preserve multilingual evidence, and allow contradiction, hybridity and abstention.

Document-level candidates such as nodal points, floating or empty signifiers, durable formations and populist frontiers may require corpus-level comparison and human validation.
