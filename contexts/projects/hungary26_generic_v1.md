# Hungary26 generic project background v1

Hungary26 is a configurable political-discourse research project for Hungarian electoral/public communication. This resource is intentionally generic and public-safe. Restricted source lists, researcher annotations, project-specific codebooks and operational settings belong in approved private overlays.

Potential analytical context includes parties, candidates, institutions, civil-society actors, media and platform-native accounts, with Hungarian-language evidence preserved wherever possible. Relevant discourse may involve political identities, demands, grievances, competing futures, institutional legitimacy, Europe, sovereignty and other project-defined themes.

Treat actor identity, organizational affiliation, party family and researcher-provided formation hints as contextual priors rather than source evidence. Analyse each current item independently and allow contradiction, hybridity and abstention.

Claims about stable formations, hegemonic articulation, empty or floating signifiers, durable political frontiers and temporal shifts generally require corpus-level comparison and human validation.
